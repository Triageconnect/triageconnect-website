About TriageConnect.ca

Built to support the people delivering emergency care.

TriageConnect was created around a practical question: can secure virtual physician capacity help Canadian hospitals improve flow for appropriate lower-acuity patients?

Our purpose

Help hospitals explore a safe, practical, and measurable new care pathway.

Emergency-department pressure affects patients, nurses, physicians, administrators, and communities.

Our role is to develop a hospital-focused virtual physician model that complements established teams and respects local governance. The objective is not to make exaggerated promises; it is to work with healthcare leaders to test what is clinically appropriate, operationally useful, and sustainable.

Hospital-ledLocal governance and approved workflows guide implementation.
Clinically responsiblePatient eligibility and escalation require professional judgment.
Canada-focusedThe model is designed for Canadian healthcare environments.
Evidence-orientedPilots should be defined, measured, and evaluated.

Our vision

A more connected front door to emergency care.

We envision hospitals having an additional, carefully governed option to connect selected lower-acuity patients with qualified physicians—while emergency teams retain oversight of the broader patient journey.
