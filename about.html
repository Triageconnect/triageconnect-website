A Canadian healthcare initiative

Helping emergency departments create safer, more flexible access to physician assessment.

TriageConnect.ca is being developed around patient safety, clinical accountability, respect for triage professionals, and measurable hospital outcomes.

M

Mission

Connect appropriate emergency department patients with qualified physicians through secure virtual care.

V

Vision

The right patient receiving the right level of care from the right provider at the right time.

P

Principles

Safety, accountability, privacy, physician-led care, hospital control, and measurable improvement.

Our approach

Build carefully. Pilot responsibly. Measure honestly.

TriageConnect does not claim to replace emergency departments or guarantee shorter waits. The model is designed to be tested through controlled partnerships with defined clinical governance and transparent outcome measurement.

Canadian focus

Programs must align with provincial licensing, privacy, credentialing, hospital policy, and applicable healthcare requirements.
