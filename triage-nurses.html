Supporting clinical judgment

An additional pathway—not a replacement for triage expertise.

TriageConnect is intended to support nurses with a clear, hospital-approved route for appropriate patients while preserving established emergency pathways for urgent and unstable cases.

Nurse-centred workflow

Triage judgment remains essential.

The triage nurse continues to identify acuity, warning signs, and eligibility according to the participating hospital’s approved criteria.

  • Clear eligibility and exclusion checklists
  • Defined escalation if a condition changes
  • Patient consent and information resources
  • Direct access to technical and operational support
  • Feedback loops to improve the workflow
RN

Designed to reduce pressure

A virtual physician pathway may help teams manage waiting-room expectations, improve access to assessment, and create a structured option during high-volume periods.

Safety first

Patients who need in-person care stay in the emergency pathway.

Urgent, unstable, or potentially life-threatening presentations are not delayed for virtual assessment. Each hospital defines escalation triggers and return-to-care procedures.
