Privacy

General website privacy information for TriageConnect.ca.

Website inquiries

Information submitted through this website is used to respond to hospital, physician, and general business inquiries. Do not submit personal health information or information about an identifiable patient.

Third-party form processing

Website forms may be processed by a third-party form-delivery service and delivered to the TriageConnect administrative email account. Before production use, TriageConnect should complete a formal privacy and security review of all website services.

Clinical services

This public website is not a clinical-care platform, does not provide medical advice, and should not be used for emergencies. Clinical privacy terms for any future hospital implementation would be governed by separate agreements, policies, and applicable law.

Contact

Privacy-related website questions may be sent to admin@triageconnect.ca.
