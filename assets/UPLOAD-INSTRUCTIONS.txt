TRIAGECONNECT PILOT WEBSITE UPDATE

Upload/replace these items in the ROOT of your GitHub repository:
- index.html
- styles.css
- script.js
- assets folder and all four images inside it

Do not delete CNAME, .gitattributes, README.md, or your other HTML pages.

After GitHub commits and Cloudflare deploys:
1. Open the website.
2. Submit one test form.
3. Check admin@triageconnect.ca for the FormSubmit activation email.
4. Click the activation/confirmation link.
5. Submit a second test form and confirm delivery.

The forms send to admin@triageconnect.ca.
