About TriageConnect

A Canadian approach to emergency department capacity.

TriageConnect was created to help hospitals respond to physician shortages, crowding and uneven demand without requiring more physical infrastructure.

Our purpose

Support emergency teams while improving access to care.

We focus on practical implementation, established clinical workflows and collaboration with hospitals, physicians and healthcare partners.

Our long-term vision is a scalable Canadian network that helps emergency departments direct the right patients to the right physician resource at the right time.

Supporting Canadian emergency departments through virtual physician capacity.
