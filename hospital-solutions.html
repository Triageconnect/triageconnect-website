Hospital Solutions

Virtual physician support designed around your emergency department.

TriageConnect complements existing operations rather than asking hospitals to replace their clinical systems.

Clinical pathway

A focused pathway for appropriate patients.

Implementation remains hospital-led and aligned with local policies, credentialing, privacy and clinical governance.

  1. 1Patients follow the hospital’s normal triage process.
  2. 2Appropriate CTAS Level 4 and 5 patients are identified.
  3. 3Patients are matched with an available emergency physician.
  4. 4Patients can be discharged to a virtual physician.
  5. 5Patients can be seen in a private room, by cell phone or virtually at home.
