We are building a network of qualified Canadian physicians interested in flexible, structured virtual care opportunities supporting participating emergency departments.

Who should apply

Experienced physicians with strong virtual clinical judgment.

  • Active provincial or territorial medical licence
  • Appropriate liability protection
  • Emergency, family, urgent care, rural, or relevant generalist experience
  • Comfort identifying limitations of virtual assessment
  • Commitment to privacy, documentation, and escalation standards
