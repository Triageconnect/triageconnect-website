Capacity is the problem.More patients do not always require more treatment rooms—they require the right physician resource at the right time.

The operational challenge

Emergency departments need another way to create capacity.

Lower-acuity patients can remain in the same queue as higher-acuity cases, increasing pressure on physicians, nurses, treatment spaces and patient flow.
