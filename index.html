How it works
1

Patient triage

An emergency department triage nurse identifies eligible patients who may benefit from virtual physician assessment instead of a full ER visit.

2

Virtual assessment

Patients connect securely with a physician for clinical evaluation, recommendation, and referral through a HIPAA-aligned telehealth session.

3

Care coordination

Physicians document the encounter, advise next steps, and coordinate follow-up care, reducing unnecessary emergency department burden.
