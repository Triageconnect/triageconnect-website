The challenge

Emergency departments need another safe pathway.

Long waits, staffing pressures, and crowded treatment areas can delay assessment and increase strain on patients and clinical teams. Some patients require timely physician evaluation but may not initially require an emergency bed or extensive in-person resources.

  • Support during peak volume and staffing shortages
  • Earlier physician access for appropriate patients
  • Reduced waiting-room pressure on triage teams
  • More focused use of in-person emergency resources
A modern response to a growing access problemVirtual assessment can complement—not replace—the emergency department.
How it works

A clear, hospital-approved workflow.

Each participating organization defines its own clinical criteria, governance, documentation, and escalation process.

1

Patient arrives

Registration and normal emergency triage begin.

2

Nurse identifies eligibility

Hospital-approved inclusion and exclusion criteria are applied.

3

Virtual physician assesses

A qualified physician reviews the case and determines next steps.

4

Right care pathway

The patient is treated, referred, followed up, or returned for in-person care.
