Clear answers

Common questions from hospitals, nurses, and physicians.

The final service model will depend on participating organizations, provincial requirements, clinical governance, and physician licensing.

Is TriageConnect a replacement for the emergency department?

No. It is intended to provide an additional physician assessment pathway for appropriate patients.

Who decides whether a patient is eligible?

The participating hospital defines the criteria, and the triage nurse applies the hospital-approved process.

What if the virtual physician believes in-person assessment is needed?

The patient is directed back into the established emergency pathway according to the hospital’s escalation protocol.

Can any physician join?

Physicians must meet licensing, credentialing, liability, privacy, and participating organization requirements.

Will this definitely reduce wait times?

No outcome should be guaranteed before it is demonstrated. Pilot programs should measure access, flow, safety, and experience.

How does a hospital get started?

Begin with an operational consultation to define the challenge, intended patient group, coverage hours, and pilot objectives.
